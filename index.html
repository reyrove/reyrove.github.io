01 — THE THREAD

I started with fibers.
Then I started asking
what happens when
fibers become
algorithms.

My work moves between textile engineering, mathematics, creative coding, generative art and AI.

I am interested in the hidden structures behind things: rhythm, repetition, tension, geometry, texture and systems.

Code becomes a kind of loom — a way to turn rules into forms, materials into experiences, and experiments into visual language.

Reyhaneh Daneshdoost / Reyrove
